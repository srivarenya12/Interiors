How a project runs

Most 2 BHK homes take 10 to 14 weeks from signing to handover.

  1. Week 1

    Visit

    We measure, listen and photograph. The visit is free.

  2. Weeks 2–3

    Concept

    Mood boards, a layout and a first cost estimate.

  3. Weeks 4–5

    Detail

    3D views, material samples and final drawings. You approve each one.

  4. Weeks 6–12

    Build

    Weekly site photos and a fixed schedule on your phone.

  5. Weeks 13–14

    Handover

    Styling, a deep clean and a snag list we close in 7 days.

What clients say

Questions we get a lot

Is the first visit really free?

Yes. We visit within Bengaluru at no charge and share a rough budget range within three days.

Can I hire you for design only?

Yes. Design-only projects start at about ₹150 per sq ft and include drawings, 3D views and a material list.

How do payments work?

Payments are tied to milestones: booking, design approval, material delivery, mid-build and handover. Nothing is due before you approve the stage.

Do you give a warranty?

We give a 5-year warranty on carpentry and a 1-year warranty on electrical and plumbing work we carry out.
